Services

Precision work, priced by the piece.

Every job is quoted individually after seeing the piece — metal, stone, and condition all affect the work involved. Below is the full range of what's done at the bench.

Stone Setting

Prong, bezel, channel, pavé, and flush settings for diamonds and colored stones, in new mountings or replacing a stone that's been lost.

Ring Sizing

Sizing rings up or down, including pieces with existing stones or engraving that need extra care during the resize.

Heirloom Restoration

Rebuilding worn shanks, replacing broken prongs, and reviving pieces passed down through a family so they're safe to wear again.

Prong & Head Repair

Re-tipping and rebuilding worn prongs before a stone is at risk of falling out — the single most common bench emergency.

Custom Mountings

One-off custom settings built around a specific stone — an engagement ring, a redesigned family stone, or a new piece from scratch.

Inspection & Cleaning

A bench check of prongs, settings, and clasps, plus a professional clean — recommended yearly for rings worn daily.

About

A bench, not a storefront — and that's the point.

Benjamin Edwards founded Edwards Setting & Restoration to do one thing well: careful, precise bench work on the jewelry people actually care about keeping. No case full of inventory to sell you on — just the tools, the loupe, and the piece in front of him.

Based in Darke County, Ohio, the shop takes on stone setting, sizing, and restoration work for local clients and for pieces mailed in from farther out. Every piece gets the same standard: it should leave the bench stronger than it arrived, with the setting done the way it should have been done the first time.
